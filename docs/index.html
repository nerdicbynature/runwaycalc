RunwayCalc

RunwayCalc calculates take-off ground run and total distance over a 50 ft obstacle from your aircraft's book values, corrected for density altitude, runway slope, surface, contamination and wind.

Estimate only. Results are not a substitute for the aircraft flight manual (AFM/POH). The pilot in command is solely responsible for verifying take-off performance.
