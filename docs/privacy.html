Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-20

1. Controller

Andre Machowiak, runwaycalc@machowiak.de

2. Summary

RunwayCalc does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. The app makes no network connections and contains no analytics, tracking or advertising.

3. Data stored on your device

Aircraft you create, your settings and your last take-off inputs are stored locally on your device only. They are not accessible to me. Deleting the app removes this data.

4. Export and import

Aircraft files are created or read only when you start an export, share or import yourself. What happens to an exported file afterwards depends on the app or service you share it with.

5. App Store

Apple may process data as the operator of the App Store under its own privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy. I have no influence on this processing.

6. Hosting of these pages

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc.). When you visit them, GitHub may process your IP address in server logs. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The app itself never contacts these pages.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR (Articles 15 to 21) you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Because I do not process personal data about you, there is generally no data to disclose. You may lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
